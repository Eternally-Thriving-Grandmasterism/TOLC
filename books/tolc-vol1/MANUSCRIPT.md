## Chapter 1 — What TOLC is

TOLC here means a **mercy-first thriving lattice**: truth, order, love, compassion, service, abundance, joy, and cosmic harmony as lived gates — not a slogan stack.

Core laws (curriculum form):

- Abundance before scarcity stories

## Chapter 4 — Daily practices

1. **Truth Mirror** (5–15 min): "What am I most afraid to admit right now?"
2. **Body Scan** (all day): Feel tightness / cold / heat → ask what truth it protects.
3. **Mercy Reset** (when hurt): "Wrong. Sorry. Better next time."
4. **Relation Act**: Treat one being as kin today.

Miss a day? Return faster. Return speed > perfection.

## Chapter 5 — Pure nuggets (body-verifiable)

1. **All is relation** — Separation is the illusion. Treat everything as kin.
2. **Truth is direct seeing** — No story, no belief. See what is.
3. **The body never lies** — Trust physical signals before mind narratives.
4. **Mercy is the only clean compiler** — Judgment creates bugs; mercy removes them.
5. **Return speed > perfection** — Notice distortion; return faster each time.
6. **Love is the original code** — Everything else is patch.
7. **Eternal thriving is possible** — By inhabiting life without distortion, not escaping it.
